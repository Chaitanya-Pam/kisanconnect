## Idea
*Problem Statement*

Farmers across India (and most developing agricultural economies) face a silent but massive financial burden: middlemen in the fertilizer and pesticide supply chain.
A fertilizer with an MRP of ₹300 is often sold to farmers for ₹800–₹900 by local dealers/middlemen.
Farmers, especially in rural areas, don't have direct access to manufacturers or distributors.
Language barriers and low digital literacy make it hard for farmers to identify the correct disease affecting their crop, and also finding the exact fertilizer/pesticide needed.
Wrong or delayed treatment leads to crop loss, and overpaying for inputs erodes already thin farm profit margins.

Core issue: Farmers lack (a) an easy way to identify what's wrong with their crop, and (b) a direct channel to buy the right product at a fair price.

*Our Solution — KisanConnect*

A simple, visual, multilingual mobile app that lets a farmer go from "my crop looks sick" to "I've called the company and ordered the right fertilizer" in three taps — with zero middleman markup.
Core Idea in One Line

"See your crop → Find the disease → Call the company directly."

*App Flow (User Journey)*

Step 1: Home Screen — Crop Selection
Grid of crop images (rice, cotton, wheat, tomato, sugarcane, etc.)
Each crop name shown in three formats: local language, English, and a picture — so literacy is never a barrier.
Farmer simply taps the picture that matches their crop.

Step 2: Disease Selection Screen
After selecting a crop, the app shows common diseases for that specific crop, again with images (leaf spots, blight, wilt, rot, etc.) plus simple descriptions/symptoms in the local language.
Farmer compares the picture/symptoms to what they see in their field and selects the matching disease.

Step 3: Fertilizer/Pesticide Recommendation Screen
Once a disease is selected, the app lists verified fertilizers/pesticides that treat it — including dosage guidance.
Each listing shows:
Product name & manufacturer
Official MRP (so the farmer knows the fair price before calling anyone)
A direct "Call Company" button connecting the farmer straight to the manufacturer/authorized distributor — no local middleman involved.

Step 4: Direct Order
Farmer calls or messages the company directly through the app, negotiates delivery, and pays MRP (or close to it) instead of the inflated middleman price.

*Unique Value Proposition*

For farmers: Fair pricing, faster diagnosis, and confidence in choosing the right treatment.
For fertilizer companies: A direct-to-consumer channel, better brand visibility, and reduced dependency on dealer networks.
For agriculture as a whole: Reduced crop loss due to faster, more accurate disease treatment.
